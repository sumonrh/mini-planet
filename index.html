Little Planet — Pocket Adventure

Explore one tiny planet. Seven worlds, seven small moments — 7 Small Wonders. Click anything (or press E once) and your hero walks over and does the whole job — chopping, foraging, building, even fighting.

  • Act: click a tree, rock, bush, beast, person or wonder — or press E once nearby. The hero auto-works it until done. Manual steering (WASD) cancels non-combat jobs.
  • Dash: Shift rolls through danger (brief invulnerability). Dodging a swipe at the last moment triggers a Clean Dodge + critical hits.
  • Levels: chopping, fighting, fishing, cooking and wonders earn XP. Level up for perks and better weapons (Rusty Hatchet → Starforged Blade).
  • Fast travel: press M for the globe, then click any shore to teleport there.
  • Life chapters: Arrival → Roots → Family → Mastery → Legacy. Each chapter unlocks a life trait. Open J for the journal story, family, technology and almanac tabs.
  • Weather: press N for the almanac. Forecasts warn you about blizzards, thunderstorms, floods, droughts, eruptions, wildfires and hail — build storm shutters, a cellar, a well, irrigation, a pump or an ash mask before they hit.
  • Research: press K to open the workshop. Earn insight by discovering things, surviving hazards and completing chapters; research tools, buildings and firearms. Build the Workshop on the homestead board (H) once researched.
  • Weapons: press L for the loadout. Melee: hatchet → iron axe → war axe → halberd. Ranged: sling → bow → crossbow → musket → lever rifle → shotgun. Right-click to aim/fire manually, X to reload, left-click a predator for auto-engage. Craft ammo at the workshop; gunshots are loud and can call wolves.
  • Bounties & hunting: press B for the bounty board. Deer and hares roam the forest and fields — hunt them for meat and pelts. A clean shot (one or two hits) yields bonus meat. Drive off poachers to free trapped animals, or confront them.
  • Tools: research upgrades the whole kit — fishing handline → rod → net → trap, farming hoe → plough → seed drill → thresher, building hammer → saw → power tools. The hero visibly carries the new rod, saw and drill.
  • Accessibility: pause menu settings include reduced flashes/shake, large text, high-contrast panels, and the Cozy difficulty.
  • Legacy: when your goals are met, retire into an heir from the homestead board. New Game+ keeps your blueprints, journal and one heirloom tool, with harsher weather.
  • Threats: bears, wolves, and night robbers, tolls and rustlers. Confront robbers to pay, fight or intimidate. Lanterns, dogs and fences deter them.
  • Fernwood: tend the eternal flame 3×.
  • Clover Fields: water and harvest the rows.
  • Sunstone Oasis: wake the oasis spring.
  • Shell Cove: ring the dune bell.
  • Tideglass Reef: light the lighthouse (you can swim!).
  • Ember Heights: scan the volcano — hold still.
  • Northlight: wake the aurora at the stones.
  • Gather: your axe is always in hand — click any pine, palm, or orchard tree and the hero fells it and splits the trunk automatically. Nodes regrow.
  • Sea skiff: follow the trail south from Shell Cove to the shore skiff. Click it (or E) to board, sail with WASD, and click/press E in open water to cast and again to reel in a 🐟. Click shore (or E) to step off.
  • Move: WASD, arrow keys, or click anywhere on the planet to walk there.
  • Survive: sprinting burns energy — eat food (F), rest by standing still, beware the arctic cold.
  • Live: hunger drains slowly now — explore first, eat later. Press H for the homestead board: build hut → farmhouse, dig a well, plant plots, raise animals.
  • Family: befriend Lena or Marina with gifts, craft a sunstone ring, marry, raise kids — every mouth adds upkeep. Hire hands, survive droughts, floods, tornadoes and wolf packs, then retire into your heir.
  • Bears & wolves: adults flash ❗ before a heavy swipe — dash away or take the hit. Click a predator once and your hero fights automatically. Pick 🌿 remedy from glowing bushes, press R to heal fast. Save anytime from the pause menu (Esc → Save Game); death returns you to your last save.
